# Cool Skate Eagle — Delete My Account

You can delete your account and all associated data in either of these ways.

## In the game

On the title screen, open Settings and tap Delete Account. Deletion takes effect right away and can't be undone.

## By email

Email us at hi@ohnogames.io and include the display name on your account. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and linked sign-in
- Display name
- Leaderboard entries
- Cloud save data
- Replay data

Some gameplay and session event logs held by PlayFab may remain until they expire.
